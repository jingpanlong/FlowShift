The FlowShift WPF interface and bridge are independently authored.
No Avalonia UI, Filee logo, updater, telemetry or Filee installer is used.
The unmodified conversion libraries and separate conversion executables
keep their own original licenses.

The full distribution includes the .NET runtime and native conversion
libraries, 7-Zip, rhwp, FFmpeg, Pandoc, Ghostscript, LibreOffice/H2Orestart/
Temurin JRE and calibre. These components are unpacked under the current
user's FlowShift runtime directory. Original license files and notices
are retained there; additional notices are in runtime/licenses.

In particular, FFmpeg, Pandoc, Ghostscript, calibre, H2Orestart and the JRE
have GPL/AGPL or other component-specific terms described in Filee's full
notices. They run as separate programs; their source projects, versions,
download URLs and pinned SHA-256 hashes are listed in the included upstream
notices and Vendor/Filee/src/Filee.Engines/Infrastructure/engines.json.

Build dependencies are supplied by NuGet and the publishers' official
release endpoints. No paid service or cloud conversion API is used.
