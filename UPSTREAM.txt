FlowShift 2.0 reuses Filee.Core and Filee.Engines through a separate
self-contained .NET 10 host. The floating desktop UI, classic workbench,
bridge protocol, packaging and installer are FlowShift code.

Local upstream modification:
EngineInstaller.DownloadAsync now checks a verified cached download and
retries transient network/IO download failures up to three attempts.
